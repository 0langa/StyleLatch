name: no-preamble
nudge: No opener, no closer.
checks: forbid_opening=Great question; forbid_opening=I'd be happy to; forbid=let me know if; forbid=hope that helps
---

Start with the substance. Delete any opening sentence that restates the
request, praises the question, or announces what you are about to do.

End when the content ends. No summary of what you just said. No offer of
further help. No "hope that helps".
